Experience Across Generations of Computing

My background is built on a long technical career spanning multiple generations of computing, from software development and databases through data engineering, data science and machine learning to current research and hands-on work in generative AI and Large Language Models.

This provides a perspective on AI grounded not only in emerging technologies, but also in the data, systems, architectures and computational foundations on which they depend.

That combination of long-term technical experience and current research is particularly useful when assessing whether a proposed AI approach is technically sound, appropriately scoped and genuinely useful.
